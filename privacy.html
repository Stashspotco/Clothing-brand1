Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Stash Spot CO. (“we”, “us”, “our”), based in Winterthur, Switzerland, collects, uses, stores and protects your personal data when you visit our website, place an order, or contact us. We are the controller responsible for your personal data. We comply with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where it applies to you, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Information we collect

Information you give us. When you create an account, place an order or get in touch, we collect your name, email address, shipping and billing address, phone number (if provided), order contents, and the messages you send us.

Payment information. Card and payment details are entered directly with our payment provider and processed by them. We do not see or store your full card number.

Information collected automatically. When you browse, we collect device and connection data such as IP address, browser type, pages viewed, referring page, and interactions, partly through cookies and similar technologies. See our Cookie Policy.

2. How we use your information

  • To process, fulfil and deliver your orders, and to take payment.
  • To manage your account and provide customer support.
  • To send order, shipping and returns updates.
  • To send marketing emails about drops and offers, where you have consented (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To prevent fraud, secure our site, and keep accurate business records.
  • To understand how our site is used and to improve our products and service.
  • To comply with legal and tax obligations.

3. Legal bases for processing

We rely on the following legal bases: performance of a contract (to fulfil your orders and account), your consent (for marketing and non-essential cookies), our legitimate interests (to secure and improve our service and prevent fraud), and compliance with a legal obligation (such as accounting and consumer law).

4. Sharing your information

We share data only with parties that help us run the store: payment processors, shipping and logistics carriers, IT and hosting providers, email and analytics providers, and professional advisers. These parties may only use your data on our instructions. We may also disclose data where required by law. We never sell your personal data.

5. International transfers

Some of our providers are located outside Switzerland or the EEA. Where data is transferred internationally, we put appropriate safeguards in place, such as adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses, so your data remains protected.

6. How long we keep your data

We keep order and transaction records for as long as required by Swiss tax and commercial law (generally up to 10 years). Account data is kept while your account is active. Marketing data is kept until you unsubscribe. We delete or anonymise data when it is no longer needed.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, email us at privacy@stashspot.co. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Switzerland, the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC); in the EU, your local data protection authority.

8. Security

We use technical and organisational measures — including encryption in transit, access controls and secure hosting — to protect your data. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

Our store is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided us data, please contact us and we will remove it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date above shows when it was last revised. Material changes will be communicated where appropriate.

11. Contact us

For any privacy question or request, contact us at privacy@stashspot.co or via our contact page. Postal: Stash Spot CO., Im Feldtal 5, 8408 Winterthur, Switzerland.
