Made properly, or not at all

Every garment is developed in our Winterthur studio and produced in a family-run factory in northern Portugal that we visit in person. We use organic and recycled fibres wherever we can, dye in small batches, and price fairly by selling direct — no middlemen, no seasonal markdowns games.

We keep our palette to black, white and grey on purpose. It’s a discipline: pieces that layer endlessly, never clash, and let the cut and the cloth do the talking. Buy less, buy better, wear it out.
