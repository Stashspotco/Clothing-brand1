Shipping

Last updated: 20 June 2026

This policy explains where we ship, how long delivery takes, and what it costs. Orders are sent with tracked, carbon-neutral shipping.

Where we ship

We currently ship across Switzerland and to most of Europe. If your country isn’t available at checkout, contact us and we’ll see what we can do.

Processing time

Orders are processed and dispatched within 1–2 business days. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are processed on the next business day. You’ll receive a dispatch email with tracking once your order ships.

Shipping costs

  • Standard shipping: CHF 8.90.
  • Free standard shipping on all orders over CHF 80.

Any costs are shown clearly at checkout before you pay.

Delivery estimates

  • Switzerland: 2–4 business days after dispatch.
  • Europe: 4–8 business days after dispatch.

These are estimates, not guarantees. Delivery can occasionally take longer due to carrier delays, weather, or customs.

Tracking

Once your order is dispatched, we’ll send a tracking link so you can follow your parcel to your door.

Customs and duties

For deliveries outside Switzerland and the EU, your order may be subject to import duties or taxes set by the destination country. These are the responsibility of the recipient and are not included in our prices or shipping costs.

Failed delivery and lost parcels

Please make sure your address is correct at checkout — we can’t be responsible for parcels sent to an incorrect address. If your tracking shows delivered but you haven’t received it, or your parcel appears lost in transit, contact us and we’ll investigate with the carrier and put it right.
