Cookie Policy

Last updated: 20 June 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Stash Spot CO. uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site work, remember your preferences, and understand how it is used. We also use similar technologies such as local storage and pixels, which we refer to collectively as “cookies”.

The categories we use

Strictly necessary

These are required for the site to function — for example remembering the items in your bag, keeping you signed in, and securing checkout. They cannot be switched off in our systems.

Preferences

These remember choices you make, such as region or display settings, to give you a more personalised experience.

Analytics

These help us understand how visitors use the site — which pages are popular and where people run into problems — so we can improve it. The information is aggregated and used for statistics.

Marketing

These are used to measure the effectiveness of our campaigns and, where you have consented, to show you more relevant content. They may be set by us or by advertising partners.

Consent

Strictly necessary cookies are always active because the site cannot work without them. For preferences, analytics and marketing cookies, we ask for your consent and you can change your choice at any time.

Third-party cookies

Some cookies are set by third parties that provide services to us, such as analytics or payment providers. Those parties process data in line with their own privacy policies.

Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings, and most browsers let you block them entirely. Note that blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop parts of the site — like the bag and checkout — from working.

Changes

We may update this policy as our use of cookies changes. Please check back for the latest version.
