Returns & Refunds

Last updated: 20 June 2026

We want you to love what you ordered. If something isn’t right, you can return it within 30 days of delivery for a refund. This policy explains how.

Return window

You have 30 days from the day your order is delivered to request a return. Returns requested after this window may not be accepted.

Condition of items

To be eligible, items must be:

  • Unworn, unwashed and undamaged.
  • In their original condition with all tags attached.
  • Returned in their original packaging where possible.

We may refuse returns that show signs of wear, washing, or damage not present at delivery.

How to return

  • Email returns@stashspot.co with your order number and the items you’d like to return.
  • We’ll reply with return instructions and a returns label.
  • Pack the items securely and send them back using the instructions provided.

Standard returns from within Switzerland are free. For international returns, the return shipping cost is the customer’s responsibility unless the item is faulty or incorrect.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your return, we’ll email you to confirm. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank or card provider. Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the item was faulty or we sent the wrong product.

Exchanges

The fastest way to get a different size or colour is to return your item for a refund and place a new order. If you’d like help, contact us and we’ll do our best to assist.

Faulty or incorrect items

If your item arrives faulty, damaged, or we sent the wrong product, contact us within 14 days of delivery with a photo. We’ll arrange a free return and a replacement or full refund, including any shipping you paid. This does not affect your statutory rights.

Sale items

Sale items can be returned under this policy unless they are marked “final sale” at the time of purchase, in which case they are not eligible for return or refund except where faulty.

Non-returnable items

Gift cards and any item marked final sale cannot be returned.

Contact us

Need help with a return? Email returns@stashspot.co or use our contact page.
